## Founding the Team

- Our Lead Facilitator and Engineer worked with teachers and district administrators to bring FIRST Tech Challenge to Azusa High School.
- We recruited 16 students from different backgrounds to launch the team and expand access to hands-on STEM opportunities in our community.
- Our founding team established a robotics program that future Azusa High School students could build on.
